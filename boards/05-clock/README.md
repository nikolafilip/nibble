# Board 05: clock

The clock, the speed knob, the RUN/STEP switch, the halt gate and the
power-on reset. 14 transistors in cells plus the two of the Schmitt trigger,
one pot, one DIP switch, one jumper.

| | |
|---|---|
| Bus signals used | in: HLT. out: CLK, RST |

- **Oscillator**: a source-coupled Schmitt trigger (Q1 and Q2 share a 1k
  source resistor; Q2's 10k load and Q1's 47k load put the source at 0.45 V
  and 0.1 V) reads the RC node X and trips at Vth + 0.55 V going up, Vth +
  0.15 V going down. Both trip points move with the same transistor's
  threshold, so the hysteresis survives any threshold spread. Its output is
  Q2's drain, inverted by SA (4.7k pull-up), which charges X through 100k plus
  the 1M pot. 47 nF on X gives roughly 500 Hz down to 40 Hz; the SLOW jumper
  adds 2.2 uF for 10 Hz down to 0.8 Hz.
- **RUN**: the switch puts +5 V through a diode and 10k onto CLK, and the
  oscillator's transistor pulls CLK low for half of each cycle. **STEP**: the
  switch is open, the transistor is held off, and the panel's CLK button owns
  the line. The diode keeps the 10k from loading the button in STEP.
- **HLT** high holds CLK low.
- **Power-on reset**: 2.2 uF charging through 100k into a Schmitt trigger
  holds RST high for about a tenth of a second after power-up.

Three oscillators failed in simulation before this one (D036): two plain
inverters with an RC settle at a stable operating point; a MOSFET astable
falls into its both-on state; and reading the Schmitt trigger at the wrong
node let the outer loop settle half way. The mixed-threshold corner found
every one of them.
