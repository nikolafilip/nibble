# Board 07: bus hub

No logic. USB-B power entry through a 750 mA polyfuse, a power LED, bulk
capacitance, the four 10k pull-ups for BUS0#..BUS3# (the only pull-ups on the
bus in the whole machine), test loops on the bus, clock and reset lines, and
eight identical 50-pin headers. Every board plugs in with a straight 50-way
ribbon cable. The USB plug is the power switch.

Generated by `sim/build_hub.py`. ERC clean.
