# Cards

The machine as 100 x 100 mm boards on one crimped ribbon (docs/cards.md, D042
to D045). Every card has the 64-pin bus header along its top edge (pin 1 at
the left), two M3 holes mid-height on the sides, 100 nF + 10 uF at the top
right, and its logic on the dense tile: 7.68 mm columns, one TO-92 per 5.08
mm row, the pull-up lying along the row above its stack, GND and +5V rails
alternating between the columns on the back. Generated by `sim/build_*_card.py`
from the gate lists in `sim/`; laid out and routed by `sim/pcb.py`. Do not
hand-edit the schematics.

| Card | Directory | Transistors | Bus signals | Neighbour link |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Register bit 0..3 | `reg0` .. `reg3` | 99 each | in: BUS<i>#, CLK, AI, AO, BI, BO, BA, AB, OI. out: A<i>, B<i>. BUS<i># driven open-drain when AO or BO | none |
| ALU bit 0..3 | `alu0` .. `alu3` | 66, 68, 68, 73 | in: A<i>, B<i>, SUB, ONE, F1, F0, EO. out: BUS<i># (open drain); bit 3 also CF, ZF | 2x3: carry C<i+1> and zero-so-far ZS<i> to bit i+1 |
| Counter bit 0..7 | `ctr0` .. `ctr7` | 62 (59 for bit 7) | in: PCL, CLK, RST (bit 0 also PCE). out: PC<i> | 2x3: count carry TC<i+1> to bit i+1; 2x6 chained ribbon from the sequencer: OPR<i>, PCR, RAI |
| Memory control | `memctl` | 59 | in: BUS3..0#, MAI, MI, MO, CLK | 2x6 chained ribbon to the slot cards: MAR3..0 and complements, MIN, MON |
| Memory slot pair | `memslot` (x8) | 91 | BUS3..0# in (MI) and out (MO, open drain) | the ribbon from memory control; jumpers A3 A2 A1 pick the pair |
| Program, four words | `prog` (one per four words) | 41 + 32 diodes | in: PC7..0. out: M7..0 through diodes | none; jumpers P7 P6 P5 P4 G3 G2 pick the addresses 4c..4c+3 |
| Clock | `clock` | 14 + the Schmitt pair | in: HLT. out: CLK, RST | none |
| Hub | `hub` | 0 | every line, on two headers | none |
| Panel A, B, C | `panela`, `panelb`, `panelc` | 28, 20, 32 | A: A3..B0, SUB..AB switched and shown, D3..0 CF ZF CLK RST shown. B: OI..MAI, MI..INP switched and shown, CLK and RST buttons. C: PC7..0, M7..0 shown, DATA switches driven onto BUS# while INP | none |
| Coupon | `coupon` | 26 | power only | none |

## Register bit cards (`reg0` .. `reg3`)

Bit i of the three registers on the bus: A and B (the ALU's operands) and
OUT (the display), as three master-slave D flip-flops with their input muxes
(A takes the bus when AI, B when AB, else holds; B takes the bus when BI, A
when BA, else holds; OUT takes the bus when OI, else holds), the bus driver,
and the card's own two-phase clock (PH1, PH2 from CLK, D033) and hold
decoders. Four designs, the bit baked in, because a bit is three bus lines
and two cards jumpered alike would fight over them. `sim/registers.build_bit`
is the same logic as board 02 sliced per bit; the boards' testbench and the
machine deck take the four cards as the group `regc`.

Verification (2026-09-10), from the kicad-cli exports of the four routed cards
on one bus (`sim/tb_registers.py cards`), the 113-step register script: every
value through A and B, A and B driven back, MOV, XCH, OUT, holds:

| Netlist | Corner | Result |
|---|---|---|
| kicad-cli exports, four cards | TYP, LO, HI | 113 / 113 each |
| kicad-cli exports, four cards | MIX seeds 1, 2, 3 | 113 / 113 each |
| gate list (`cards@dev`) | TYP, LO, HI, MIX 1, 2, 3 | 113 / 113 each |

Machine gate (2026-09-11): `sim/gate.py --boards alu,hub,regc`, all twelve
programs at TYP with the four cards in the deck, 0 mismatches each (fib 127
ticks, calc 616, lfsr 1497, sort 974, mul 314, the rest between).

Layout: each card routes DRC-clean in one freerouting pass (0 unconnected),
ERC clean, 10.3 of 11 tile columns used.

Reproduce from `sim/`:

```
python3 build_reg_card.py 0                    # and 1, 2, 3
<kicad python> pcb.py ../cards/reg0 reg0
python3 tb_registers.py cards out/tb_regcards --corner MIX --seed 2
python3 machine.py programs/fib.asm --boards alu,hub,regc --corner TYP
```

## ALU bit cards (`alu0` .. `alu3`)

Bit i of the ALU: the full adder/subtractor bit (B xor SUB, carry in from
bit i-1), AND, OR, XOR selected by F1 F0, and the open-drain bus driver
while EO. Bit 0 starts the carry with SUB and the zero chain; bit 3 ends
them in CF and ZF on the bus. The carry and zero-so-far run over a 2x3 IDC
link between neighbouring cards (`frame.card_links`: IN at the bottom left,
OUT at the bottom right). `sim/alu.build_bit` is board 01 sliced per bit;
the group `aluc` stands for the four cards in the machine deck.

Verification, from the kicad-cli exports of the four routed cards chained by
their links (`sim/tb_alu.py cards --cases exhaustive`): every A, B, SUB, ONE,
F1 F0 combination, 2304 cases.

| Netlist | Corner | Result |
|---|---|---|
| kicad-cli exports, four cards | TYP | 2304 / 2304, worst settle 54 us |
| kicad-cli exports, four cards | LO | 2304 / 2304, worst settle 28 us |
| kicad-cli exports, four cards | HI | 2304 / 2304, worst settle 90 us |
| kicad-cli exports, four cards | MIX seed 1 | 2304 / 2304, worst settle 69 us |
| gate list (`cards@dev`) | TYP | 2304 / 2304 |

Layout: alu0 and alu3 route in one freerouting pass; alu1 and alu2 needed
the 300-pass cap and the 0.6 mm via (their last net drops from the bus
header into the first column). All four DRC clean, 0 unconnected.

## Counter bit cards (`ctr0` .. `ctr7`)

Bit i of the program counter (count when PCE, load the jump target OPR<i>
when PCL, load the return address RA<i> when PCR, clear on RST) and of the
return register RA (a transparent latch open while RAI and PH1). The count
carry TC<i+1> = TC<i> and PC<i> runs over the 2x3 links; the sequencer's
operand and load lines come down a chained 2x6 ribbon (`frame.card_ribbon`),
each card taking its own OPR line. Group `ctrc`.

Verification: `call.asm` on the eight cards from the gate lists, 0
mismatches; the machine gate with the exports is in the commit that closes
the counter cards.

## Memory control and slot cards (`memctl`, `memslot`)

Control: the four-bit address register MAR (transparent latches open while
MAI and PH1) and the write and read gates MIN (low while MI and PH1, so a
write ends at the clock edge) and MON (low while MO), sent to the slot
cards over a chained 2x6 ribbon with MAR and its complements. Slot pair:
two 4-bit slots (even: MAR0 = 0, odd: MAR0 = 1) of the pair the three
jumpers select (A3 A2 A1: the centre pin to '1' where the address bit is 1),
eight cross-coupled cells with their write and read pull-downs, and an LED
per cell. One design, eight cards, jumpered 0 to 7. Group `memc`.

The jumpers bring MAR1..3 (or their complements) onto the card, so in the
deck they are ports of the slot subcircuit (`machine.ports_of`); left
inside they float, and every read came from slot pair 7.

Verification, `sim/tb_memory.py cards`: three fills of all sixteen slots
and reads back, single writes between reads, bus noise while nothing loads,
217 steps.

| Netlist | Corner | Result |
|---|---|---|
| gate lists (`cards@dev`) | TYP | 217 / 217 |

The exports at TYP, LO, HI, MIX: see the commit that closes the memory cards.

## Program card (`prog`)

Four words of eight DIP switches; a closed switch pulls its M line high
through a diode while the word's row is selected. Six 1x3 jumpers strap the
card to its addresses: P7..P4 the page (PC7..4), G3 G2 the word group
(PC3..2), the centre pin to '1' where the address bit is 1; PC1..0 pick the
word. A program of n words takes ceil(n/4) cards (`list` 6, `sort` 20). In
the deck (`progc`) one copy per four words is instantiated with its jumpers
and switches set from the assembled program; `prog.map.json` says which
diode is word w bit i.

Verification: `fib.asm` on the card gate lists with the ALU and hub, 0
mismatches; the whole-machine gate is in the commit that closes the cards.

## Clock card (`clock`)

Board 05's circuit on a card: the source-coupled Schmitt RC oscillator with
the speed pot and SLOW jumper, the RUN/STEP switch, the halt gate, the
power-on reset (`sim/clock.py`, drawn by `build_clock_card.py`). Deck name
`clkc`.

## Hub card (`hub`)

Two bus headers, one along the top edge and one along the bottom, one per
column face's ribbon (D043), the 64 lines routed between them. Ground is
wired, not poured: a ring above the top header's pads, down the right edge
and below the bottom header's, with bars for the capacitors, the LED and
the M pull-downs (the lines cut a pour to islands). USB-B through a 750 mA
polyfuse, the four bus pull-ups, the eight M-line pull-downs, a power LED,
100 uF, test loops on BUS3..0, CLK and RST. Deck name `hubc`.

## Panel cards (`panela`, `panelb`, `panelc`)

A: DIP-8 level switches for A3..A0 B3..B0 and SUB EO AI AO BI BO BA AB
(10k to +5V: a switch left on never fights a card pulling low), LEDs for
those lines, for the bus data (an inverter per BUS<i>#), CF, ZF, CLK and
RST. B: switches and LEDs for OI IO II PCE PCL FI HLT MAI and MI MO WRL
WRH ONE F0 F1 INP, and the CLK and RST buttons with their RC debounce and
Schmitt triggers. C, the operator's card: PC7..0 and M7..0 LEDs, the four
DATA switches and the input port (driven onto BUS# while INP). Every
switched or observed line has a 1Meg pull-down on the tile (`PD` cells)
except M, pulled down on the hub. The LEDs read row by row like the old
panel: a tile column holds one panel column (three LEDs on A, two on B and
C). Group `pnl`.

## Coupon card (`coupon`)

The gate cell in copper, powered from the bus header: ring oscillator,
fan-out 1 vs 10, 3-input NAND and NOR, a bus driver with the hub-style
pull-up, an LED; inputs on a 1x4 header with pull-downs, test loops
(`sim/coupon.py`). Its own testbench sheet is excluded from the board.
