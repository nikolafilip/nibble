# Cards

The machine as 100 x 100 mm boards on one crimped ribbon (docs/cards.md, D042
to D045). Every card has the 64-pin bus header along its top edge (pin 1 at
the left), two M3 holes mid-height on the sides, 100 nF + 10 uF at the top
right, and its logic on the dense tile: 7.68 mm columns, one TO-92 per 5.08
mm row, the pull-up lying along the row above its stack, GND and +5V rails
alternating between the columns on the back. Generated by `sim/build_*_card.py`
from the gate lists in `sim/`; laid out and routed by `sim/pcb.py`. Do not
hand-edit the schematics.

| Card | Directory | Transistors | Bus signals |
|---|---|---|---|
| Register bit 0..3 | `reg0` .. `reg3` | 99 each | in: BUS<i>#, CLK, AI, AO, BI, BO, BA, AB, OI. out: A<i>, B<i>. BUS<i># driven open-drain when AO or BO |

## Register bit cards (`reg0` .. `reg3`)

Bit i of the three registers on the bus: A and B (the ALU's operands) and
OUT (the display), as three master-slave D flip-flops with their input muxes
(A takes the bus when AI, B when AB, else holds; B takes the bus when BI, A
when BA, else holds; OUT takes the bus when OI, else holds), the bus driver,
and the card's own two-phase clock (PH1, PH2 from CLK, D033) and hold
decoders. Four designs, the bit baked in, because a bit is three bus lines
and two cards jumpered alike would fight over them. `sim/registers.build_bit`
is the same logic as board 02 sliced per bit; the boards' testbench and the
machine deck take the four cards as the group `regc`.

Verification (2026-09-10), from the kicad-cli exports of the four routed cards
on one bus (`sim/tb_registers.py cards`), the 113-step register script: every
value through A and B, A and B driven back, MOV, XCH, OUT, holds:

| Netlist | Corner | Result |
|---|---|---|
| kicad-cli exports, four cards | TYP, LO, HI | 113 / 113 each |
| kicad-cli exports, four cards | MIX seeds 1, 2, 3 | 113 / 113 each |
| gate list (`cards@dev`) | TYP, LO, HI, MIX 1, 2, 3 | 113 / 113 each |

Machine gate (2026-09-11): `sim/gate.py --boards alu,hub,regc`, all twelve
programs at TYP with the four cards in the deck, 0 mismatches each (fib 127
ticks, calc 616, lfsr 1497, sort 974, mul 314, the rest between).

Layout: each card routes DRC-clean in one freerouting pass (0 unconnected),
ERC clean, 10.3 of 11 tile columns used.

Reproduce from `sim/`:

```
python3 build_reg_card.py 0                    # and 1, 2, 3
<kicad python> pcb.py ../cards/reg0 reg0
python3 tb_registers.py cards out/tb_regcards --corner MIX --seed 2
python3 machine.py programs/fib.asm --boards alu,hub,regc --corner TYP
```
