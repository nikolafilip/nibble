# Gate coupon

26 transistors on a small board: the cell in copper, measured before any other
board is trusted. Power on J1 (5 V), inputs on J2, scope probes on the test loops.

| Test point | What it measures |
|---|---|
| TP_RING | 5-inverter ring oscillator, buffered. Gate delay = period / 10 (simulated period 14 µs) |
| TP_FO1 | Inverter output driving 1 gate. Rise time at fan-out 1 (simulated 4 µs to 2.5 V) |
| TP_FO10 | Inverter output driving 10 gates. Rise time at fan-out 10 (simulated 28 µs) |
| TP_NAND | 3-input NAND, three transistors in series. Output low with N1=N2=N3=1 (simulated 0.9 mV) |
| TP_NOR | 3-input NOR. Output low (simulated 0.1 mV) |
| TP_BUS | Open-drain driver on IN with the hub's 10k pull-up |
