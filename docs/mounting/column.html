The four faces, to scale

Each face is 460 mm between the uprights: four columns of cards at a 110 mm pitch and seven rows at the same pitch. A card's two holes are mid-height on its sides, so one rail per row carries the whole row. The sequencer (260 × 310) takes the top three rows of the front on two rails of its own. Ribbon A runs from the hub up the front and then up the right face; ribbon B from the hub's second header up the left and then the back.

How a card mounts

Cross-section through one rail, looking along it: the card hangs on two standoffs, one at each side hole.

  1. Frame. Four 900 mm uprights of 2020 extrusion at the corners of a 500 mm square, joined top and bottom by 460 mm rails with corner brackets. Rubber feet or a 500 mm plywood square under it.
  2. Rails. One 460 mm rail per row of cards, clamped to the uprights with corner brackets at the height of the row's holes: 110, 220, ... 770 mm above the base. The sequencer's three rows have two rails of their own, 256 mm apart, and no others.
  3. Cards. An M3 T-nut slides into the rail slot at each of the card's two side holes. A 12 mm M3 standoff screws into the T-nut; the card screws onto the standoff. Two points on one rail hold a 100 g card; nothing is drilled, so a card moves with two T-nuts.
  4. Hub. A card like the others, bottom right of the front, with a header on its top edge (ribbon A) and one on its bottom edge (ribbon B) and the USB socket on its left edge.
  5. Cables. Every card's 64-way header is a shrouded IDC socket along its top edge, at the same place on every card. The ribbon lies flat over the cards' top edges along a row, a socket crimped on wherever there is a card, folds down 110 mm at the row's end and comes back along the next row; at the face's top it folds round the upright onto the next face.
  6. Power. One USB cable into the hub's socket from a phone charger.

Rails and ribbons

Rails measured from the floor to the rail centre. A ribbon's length is its zigzag over the rows it serves plus the folds and 200 mm of slack at the hub.

What it does not do

The ribbons show: each one lies over a row of cards, folds down at the row's end and comes back. They are the visible wiring of the machine. It does not put the panel at standing height: on the floor the switches are at 20 to 40 cm, so it wants a low table, or the panel cards moved up a row. With all four faces used, half the program is behind whatever you stand in front of.
